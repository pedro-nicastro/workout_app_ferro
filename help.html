Frequently asked questions

How do I record a workout?

Open Today, enter the load for each exercise, and mark at least one exercise complete. Loads are saved automatically; wait for the “Saved” status before leaving the page.

How do I change kg and lb?

Open Profile → Units. The app converts values for display and keeps workout data stored in kilograms.

Why did a notification not appear?

Enable the notification option in Profile and allow notifications in your browser. FERRO can send reminders only while the app is open.

Contact

The contact button opens your email app with a FERRO support subject. Add the recipient address before sending.
